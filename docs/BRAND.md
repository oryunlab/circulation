# Oryun Lab brand

One knot, three lights. *Five, held in one line, becoming what lies between.*

Live reference: https://circulatorysensing.com/brand/ · Files: [`public/brand/`](../public/brand/)

## The mark

A five-petal knot drawn as one unbroken line, inside a tilted orbit.

- **Curve:** z(t) = e^{it} + 0.78·e^{−4it}, five petals, Wood petal pointing up, petals in generating order (상생) clockwise.
- **Line ("Current"):** one filled line, a hairline where it crosses the centre and full at each tip. Colour flows along it, held at each tip and blending through the centre.
- **Orbit:** an ellipse (rx 96, ry 30, tilted −20°) that passes behind the knot and then in front of it. "Depth" ring: thin and faint at the back, full in front.
- Thick lines are drawn as filled outlines, never as chains of short strokes (their scalloped edges read as dots).

## Three layers

| Layer | Meaning | In the mark |
|---|---|---|
| 五 form | five people, five elements, five senses; 오 begins with ㅇ, a circle | five petals |
| 輪 · 倫 line | 五輪 five rings, 五倫 five rules: circle and bond | one line through the shared centre, held in an orbit |
| 間 colour | what the five become when they meet | in-between colours, different at each level |

## Colours

Night is the primary ground (#0a0d11 to #101418). Paper versions keep the same hue, stay vivid and are darkened only until they hold 3:1 on paper (#fbf9f4). Never mix them toward grey or ink: they go muddy.

**Oryun Lab: 간색**, the in-between colours of Korean colour tradition, each holding its own element's 정색.

## Rules

- Night first; paper versions for print and light documents.
- One level at a time: an object carries the mark of the level it belongs to.
- Never recolour a single petal, stretch the mark, or drop the orbit from the full mark.
- The Five Elements are a cultural coordinate system here, not medical advice.
