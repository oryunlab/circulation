Circulation Toolkit

A portable toolkit for running Circulation workshops anywhere: twelve participant kits that sense touch and pulse and talk to each other over Wi-Fi, a facilitator case for power, network and repair, and printable worksheets. Everything packs into two cases. Each kit is one well (井); together they meet in the Hap (合 · 합 · hợp), the centre of Circulation.

Inside one participant box

A compartment box about 20 × 13 × 5 cm, numbered 01–12. Everything plugs in. The lid label carries the kit number, the five element colours and a QR code to this page.

Press → light

FSR + NeoPixel stick. One finger pad of the Circulation cup, in your element's colour.

Pulse → light

MAX30102 + NeoPixel. Light that beats with your pulse.

Touch → buzz

Coin motor. Feel someone else's press arrive.

Three kits → one centre

Wi-Fi on the XIAO ESP32-S3. Kits share readings through the travel router, so a table of participants becomes a small Circulation.

The kit circuit

One XIAO ESP32-S3 per kit. Pins follow Seeed's D-pin map; the full netlist and a prompt for drawing the schematic in Flux.ai are in FLUX_SCHEMATIC.md.
